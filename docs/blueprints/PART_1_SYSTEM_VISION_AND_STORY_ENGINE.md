## 1. Executive System Vision & Product Philosophy

### 1.1 The Bubbleverse Mission
Bubbleverse was engineered to solve a fundamental emotional and technological challenge in modern family entertainment: **transforming a child into the authentic, heroic protagonist of their own physical and digital storybook**, without sacrificing literary quality, visual consistency, cultural authenticity, or personal data privacy.

While legacy personalized book companies rely on generic pre-drawn illustrated templates where only a text name or hair color is swapped (e.g., Wonderbly, Hooray Heroes), and first-generation "AI toy apps" produce chaotic, distorted illustrations with shifting facial likeness and broken typography (e.g., Magical Hekaya, generic GPT wrappers), Bubbleverse introduces the **Artisan-Directed AI Architecture**. 

### 1.2 The "Artisan-Directed AI" Paradigm vs. Industry Alternatives

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    THE CHILDREN'S BOOK SPECTRUM                                     |
+------------------------------------+----------------------------------+----------------------------+
| 1. Legacy Manual Anti-AI           | 2. Uncontrolled "AI Spam"         | 3. BUBBLEVERSE             |
|    (Wonderbly, Hooray Heroes)      |    (Generic Wrappers, Bots)      |    (Artisan-Directed AI)   |
+------------------------------------+----------------------------------+----------------------------+
| • Anti-AI marketing stance         | • Pure automated black-box       | • Human-in-the-loop studio |
| • Zero facial customization        | • Facial likeness morphs/drifts  | • Master Character Sheet   |

| • High price ($40 - $100+)         | • Zero print preflight/margins   | • Zero-retention privacy   |
| • 10-20 business day shipping      | • Low perceived emotional value  | • 3-7 day localized delivery|
+------------------------------------+----------------------------------+----------------------------+
```

1. **Facial & Character Invariance:** Rather than prompting an AI model naively per scene (which causes the child's age, race, hair texture, and clothing to mutate on every turn), Bubbleverse anchors every illustrated book to a mathematically coherent **Master Character Sheet (MCS)**.
2. **Cultural & Linguistic Authenticity:** Arabic is treated as a first-class language. The system features full Right-to-Left (RTL) typography, native shaping, diacritic respect, and regional cultural resonance (e.g., Ramadan lanterns, Eid morning celebrations, Kaaba pilgrimages, sibling hero adventures, and nursery graduation keepsakes) alongside natural English (LTR) and bilingual editions.
3. **Zero-Retention Child Photo Privacy:** A foundational ethical pillar. Reference photos uploaded by parents are encrypted, stored in an isolated private client/vault boundary, strictly prohibited from training public AI models, never used in marketing or advertisements, and purged upon order fulfillment.
4. **Physical-First Production Standards:** Every digital generation is framed, composed, and rendered to meet **Amazon KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing) and local digital offset print specifications** (`8.5" x 8.5"` square format at 300 DPI, full bleed `2588 x 2625 px`).

## 2. Core Story Engine Logic & Narrative Architecture

### 2.1 Narrative Structure & Pacing Dynamics
Children's storybooks fail when pacing is erratic. Bubbleverse enforces strict dramatic arcs across its two standardized page-count formats:

```
Pacing Arc: 15-Page vs. 25-Page Standard Book
100% |                                      [Climax / Epiphany]
     |                                          Page 11 (15p) / Page 18 (25p)
 75% |                            [Rising Action]
     |                               Page 6-9 (15p) / Page 10-16 (25p)
 50% |             [Inciting Incident]
     |                 Page 3-4 (15p) / Page 5-7 (25p)
 25% | [World Setup]                                              [Celebration & Moral Return]
     |  Page 1-2 (15p) / Page 1-3 (25p)                              Page 14-15 (15p) / Page 24-25 (25p)
  0% +-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------->
```

#### The 15-Page Standard Edition Arc (Target: Ages 2–6)
- **Pages 1–2 (The Known World & Hero DNA):** Introduction of the child in their familiar setting, highlighting their distinctive real-world personality trait (curiosity, kindness, love of building, energetic play).
- **Pages 3–4 (The Call to Adventure & Magical Threshold):** Discovery of the central wonder (a mysterious cosmic bubble, a wandering baby dinosaur, a missing crescent moon, an animal needing help).
- **Pages 5–8 (Exploration, Companionship & Rising Tests):** The child enters the fantastical environment. If a Co-Hero (sibling or best friend) is present, teamwork dynamics are introduced here.
- **Pages 9–11 (The Central Challenge / Climax):** The obstacle is faced. Crucially, the obstacle is resolved through emotional intelligence, kindness, or cleverness—never violence.
- **Pages 12–13 (The Joyous Breakthrough):** The world is restored, illuminated, or healed. The child realizes their inner superpower.
- **Pages 14–15 (Bedtime Integration & Lasting Keepsake):** Return to comfort, parental embrace, or cozy bedtime reflection, leaving the reader with a permanent moral anchor.

#### The 25-Page Deluxe Edition Arc (Target: Ages 5–10)
Expands the 15-page framework with a multi-stage quest structure, including two secondary challenges, deeper character dialogue, richer world-building spreads, and dedicated character intros/outros.

### 2.2 The 5-Block KDP 8.5" x 8.5" Scene Prompt Formula

To guarantee that generative rendering engines (Nano Banana / Gemini, Midjourney, or Flux) produce illustrations compatible with commercial printing, every scene prompt generated by the engine is constructed using a strict 5-block compositional schema:

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                          THE 5-BLOCK SCENE PROMPT ARCHITECTURE                                   |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Block 1: Master Character DNA Anchor                                                             |
|          Exact age, gender, facial ethnicity, hair texture/color, signature theme outfit.        |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Block 2: Scene Action & Facial Emotion                                                           |
|          Specific dynamic kinetic movement, eye line, posture, and joyous micro-expression.       |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Block 3: Setting, Environment & Atmospheric Depth                                                |
|          Rich whimsical backdrop, architectural/botanical/cosmic details, depth-of-field cues.    |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Block 4: Artistic Style, Volumetric Lighting & Cinematography                                    |
|          Engineered style tokens (Pixar 3D / DreamWorks CGI / Warm Watercolor), golden hour/glow.|
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Block 5: KDP Compositional Guardrails & Negative Constraints                                     |
|          75% center safe zone directive, 25% bottom text reserve, strict anti-artifact negative. |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

#### Detailed Breakdown of the 5 Blocks:

1. **Block 1: Master Character DNA Anchor**
   - Injects the normalized attributes from `CharacterDna`.
   - *Example:* `"Master character sheet continuity: a joyful 6-year-old Egyptian boy named Zain, warm hazel-brown almond eyes, tousled wavy dark brown hair, wearing a junior astronaut jumpsuit with planetary mission patches, cosmic navy trims, and silver moon boots."`
2. **Block 2: Scene Action & Facial Emotion**
   - Describes the active physical beat, forbidding static "smiling at camera" poses.
   - *Example:* `"Leaping enthusiastically over a luminescent crystal crater, arms outstretched in pure excitement, laughing with wide wonder as he catches a floating golden star."`
3. **Block 3: Setting, Environment & Atmospheric Depth**
   - Builds immersive, believable environments with atmospheric layers (foreground, midground, background).
   - *Example:* `"On the vibrant violet surface of the Lost Bubble Galaxy, surrounded by floating iridescent star-bubbles, friendly robotic rovers, and swirling turquoise planetary rings in the deep cosmic sky."`
4. **Block 4: Artistic Style, Volumetric Lighting & Cinematography**
   - Dictates the rendering engine's physical parameters.
   - *Example:* `"Award-winning modern 3D Pixar animation style, rich subsurface scattering on skin, volumetric warm starlight illumination, cinema 4D octane render, wide angle 24mm lens perspective, crisp focus."`
5. **Block 5: KDP Compositional Directives & Negative Constraints**
   - *Composition Directive:* `"Keep the child's face and important details strictly inside the center 75% safe zone (away from 0.375\" KDP 8.5\"×8.5\" trim edges). Leave softer darker space near the bottom 25% for storybook text card."`
   - *Negative Directive:* `"No text, no letters, no speech bubbles, no watermark, no borders, no cropped head, no extra fingers, no distorted limbs."`

### 2.3 Strict Separation of Pipeline Concerns
Bubbleverse enforces a hard boundary between the three core disciplines of story creation:

```
[Parent / Child Inputs]
          │
          ▼
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│     1. NARRATIVE DOMAIN      │ ──► TextProvider Port (Gemini 2.5 / Edge Function)
│   (Plot, Dialogue, Beats)    │     Outputs: Structured JSON { pages: [{ text, action, setting }] }
└──────────────┬───────────────┘
               │
               ▼
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│     2. VISUAL DOMAIN         │ ──► stage2PromptEngine (Nano Banana / Midjourney / Flux)
│ (DNA, Style, Scene Prompts)  │     Outputs: 5-Block 300 DPI Prompts + Master Character Sheet
└──────────────┬───────────────┘
               │
               ▼
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│    3. COMPOSITION DOMAIN     │ ──► kdpCanvasRenderer & binaryPdfWriter
│ (Trim, DPI, Typography, Bleed)│     Outputs: Verified 8.5"x8.5" PDF at 2588x2625px (300 DPI)
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Text Domain:** Owns linguistic grammar, emotional rhythm, child age-vocabulary constraints, and Arabic/English phonetics. The text engine never generates visual rendering jargon.
2. **Visual Domain:** Owns character identity locking, spatial composition, theme outfits, lighting, and rendering syntax. It receives narrative action beats and transforms them into engine-specific prompt packs.
3. **Physical Layout Domain:** Owns PDF bounding boxes, bleeds (`0.125"`), safe zones (`0.375"`), font embedding, text overlays, and KDP print verification. It is completely decoupled from how the image or text was generated.

### 3.1 The Curated Catalog Pipeline (E-Commerce Fast-Track)

#### Core Philosophy & User Experience
The Catalog Pipeline represents **80% of projected B2C sales volume**. It mimics a luxury consumer e-commerce experience (like Wonderbly or Apple), eliminating creative anxiety for parents who want a guaranteed, stress-free gift.

#### Key Mechanics:
1. **Curated, Proven Stories:** The customer browses pre-authored, culturally validated master titles in `catalogStories.ts` (e.g., `zain-bubble-galaxy`, `ramadan-lantern-moon`, `sibling-super-team`, `little-graduate-star`, `animal-doctor`).
2. **Instant Live Personalization (`LiveBookPreviewCard`):** 
   - As soon as the parent enters the child's name, co-hero name, and dedication message on the Product Detail Page (`ProductDetail.tsx`), the **Live 3D Book Cover and Interior Dedication Page render in real-time** on an interactive canvas.
   - If a photo is uploaded, it is framed inside a glowing circular hero medallion on the cover and dedication spread.
   - **Zero waiting, zero spinners, zero login walls.**
3. **Ecosystem Add-on Upsells (AOV Expansion):**
   - Right on the PDP, parents can select high-margin companion items:
     - *Matching Personalized Coloring Book* (`+75 EGP / +19 SAR/AED`)
     - *Luxury Gift Box with Printed Dedication Card* (`+95 EGP / +25 SAR/AED`)
     - *24-Sticker Customized Character Sheet* (`+65 EGP / +18 SAR/AED`)
     - *Back-Cover Printed QR Audio Narration* (`+110 EGP / +29 SAR/AED`)
     - *Co-Hero Sibling / Best Friend Add-on* (`+80 EGP / +20 SAR/AED`)
4. **Frictionless Guest Checkout:**
   - Orders are placed as a guest via `GuestCheckoutModal.tsx`.
   - Requires only customer name, WhatsApp phone number, shipping address, and a 50% deposit via local rails (`InstaPay`, `Vodafone Cash`, `STC Pay`, `Mada`, or `Apple Pay`).
5. **Admin Studio Ingestion:**
   - The order arrives in `AdminOrders.tsx` with full metadata and photo references securely linked via `photoVault`.
   - Clicking **"Open in Story Studio"** immediately hydrates `AdminStudio.tsx`. Because the master plot beats are pre-authored, the system automatically binds the child's DNA and generates the complete 15-to-25 page production prompt pack in under 5 seconds.

### 3.2 The Bespoke Wizard Pipeline (Custom Co-Authoring)

#### Core Philosophy & User Experience
The Bespoke Wizard Pipeline is designed for parents, educators, or gift-givers who desire a **100% unique, custom narrative** crafted around a specific real-world event (e.g., overcoming fear of the dark, adjusting to a new baby sibling, hospital courage, or moving to a new city).

#### Step-by-Step Architectural Execution:
1. **Step 1: Child Identity & Vault Registration (`CharacterUpload.tsx`)**
   - Captures child name, age bracket (`2-4`, `5-7`, `8-10`), gender, and high-resolution reference photo.
   - The photo is normalized client-side and saved into `photoVault` (`photoVault.ts`), returning a secure, non-public vault key.
2. **Step 2: World Interests, Values & Theme Selection**
   - The user selects core interests (e.g., Space, Paleontology, Marine Biology, Inventions) and moral values (Courage, Empathy, Sharing, Curiosity).
3. **Step 3: Plot Formulation (Automatic vs. Custom Plot)**
   - *Mode A (AI-Enhanced Premise):* The system selects an algorithmic premise matching the selected theme.
   - *Mode B (Custom Freeform Plot):* The parent types a custom narrative prompt (e.g., *"My daughter Sarah is shy on her first day at Al-Zuhour Nursery, but she helps her teacher find a lost kitten"*).
4. **Step 4: Edge Function Narrative Synthesis (`generate-story`)**
   - The client invokes the Supabase Edge Function `supabase/functions/generate-story`.
   - The function validates input length, checks rate limits, and dispatches the prompt through `TextProvider` to Google Gemini.
   - The prompt enforces child development guidelines, vocabulary limits, and returns a verified JSON schema containing title, summary, character Bible, and exact page-by-page beats (`Stage2StoryBeatPage[]`).
5. **Step 5: Atomic Database Persistence (`save_generated_story` RPC)**
   - The generated story is committed to PostgreSQL in a single atomic transaction.
   - The transaction verifies caller ownership, snapshots the input configuration, inserts the story, characters, and pages, and stores an idempotency hash.
   - If any step fails, the entire transaction rolls back—preventing orphaned records.
6. **Step 6: Interactive Story Preview & Reading Room (`StoryPreview.tsx`)**
   - The parent reads the full generated text spread by spread.
   - Once satisfied, they click **"Order Physical Keepsake"**, which transfers the custom story into the Cart Drawer for guest checkout.

## 4. Character DNA & Dynamic Theme Mechanics

### 4.1 The `CharacterDna` Specification
At the heart of Bubbleverse's visual continuity is the `CharacterDna` contract defined in `src/features/studio/stage2PromptEngine.ts`:

```typescript
export interface CharacterDna {
  childName: string;                  // Primary protagonist name
  childAge: string;                   // Age in years or age band (e.g. "6", "5-7")
  childGender: 'boy' | 'girl';        // Informs physical tailoring and linguistic pronouns
  hairAndFeatures: string;            // Physical anchor: hair curl/texture, skin tone, eye color
  signatureOutfit: string;            // Thematically calibrated clothing description
  artStyle: string;                  // Rendering token: Pixar 3D, DreamWorks, Watercolor
  midjourneyCrefUrl?: string;         // Hosted URL of Master Character Sheet for --cref
  storyTheme?: string;                // Detected or selected theme key (e.g. 'space', 'dinosaurs')
  signatureProp?: string;             // Thematic item held or used (e.g. telescope, magnifying glass)
  pose2Description?: string;          // Dynamic 3/4 action pose description on the MCS
}
```

### 4.3 Algorithmic Theme Detection & DNA Hydration

When an order arrives in the Admin Studio, the operator does not need to manually configure outfits or props. The engine runs a deterministic heuristic pass via `detectStoryTheme()` and `resolveThemeDna()`:

```typescript
// Heuristic scan across story title, custom plot, gift message, and catalog ID:
export function detectStoryTheme(input: {
  storyTitle?: string;
  customPlot?: string;
  catalogId?: string;
  notes?: string;
}): StoryThemeKey {
  const corpus = `${input.storyTitle ?? ''} ${input.customPlot ?? ''} ${input.catalogId ?? ''} ${input.notes ?? ''}`.toLowerCase();
  
  // Scans multilingual keyword dictionaries (Arabic & English)
  for (const preset of THEME_PRESETS) {
    if (preset.keywords.some(keyword => corpus.includes(keyword.toLowerCase()))) {
      return preset.id;
    }
  }
  return 'bubble'; // Default signature cosmic identity
}
```

Once the theme is resolved, `resolveThemeDna()` tailors the outfit strictly to the child's gender and art style, dynamically injecting the resulting parameters into the Master Character Sheet prompt:

```
[Master Character Sheet Prompt Formulation]
"Character turn-around model sheet of [childName], a [childAge]-year-old [childGender] with [hairAndFeatures].
Wearing [signatureOutfit], holding [signatureProp].
Showing 3 views side-by-side on clean neutral studio background:
(1) front full-body standing smiling,
[pose2Description],
(3) side profile view.
Consistent facial likeness, same character in all 3 poses, [artStyle], 8k octane render."
```

### 4.4 Triple Engine Prompt Translation Architecture

Different production studios and generative models require radically different prompt syntaxes. Bubbleverse provides instantaneous, 1-click translation across the three leading generative engines:

```
                                [Master Scene Model Beat]
                                            │
               ┌────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────┐
               ▼                            ▼                            ▼
     [1. NANO BANANA / GEMINI]       [2. MIDJOURNEY v6]            [3. FLUX.1 KONTEXT]
     -------------------------       ------------------            -------------------
     • Direct descriptive tokens    • Parameterized flags         • Weight-balanced tokens
     • Explicit physical anchors     • --cref [MCS_URL] --cw 80    • Direct trigger tokens
     • Natural language lighting     • --ar 1:1 --v 6.0            • High prompt fidelity
     • Ideal for rapid studio MVP    • Commercial print gold-std   • Open-source local weights
```

#### 1. Nano Banana / Gemini Engine (`nano_banana`):
- Relies on pure, high-density descriptive natural language.
- Directly references physical facial features, exact clothing textures, and atmospheric lighting in standard paragraph syntax.
- Ideal for fast, direct API execution via Google Gemini / Imagen.

#### 2. Midjourney Engine (`midjourney`):
- Formats prompts for Midjourney v6.0 Discord/Web automation.
- Automatically appends the critical Character Reference flag: `--cref [CharacterSheetURL] --cw 80`.
  - Setting `--cw 80` (Character Weight 80) preserves the child's facial identity, skin tone, and hair structure while allowing flexible dynamic body poses across different scenes.
- Enforces square aspect ratio (`--ar 1:1`), modern artistic parameters, and negative prompts (`--no text, watermark, borders`).

#### 3. Flux.1 Kontext / LoRA Engine (`flux`):
- Formatted for local or cloud Flux.1 installations.
- Employs front-weighted token structures and prompt prioritization, optimizing for high text-prompt adherence and fine-grained spatial control.

+-------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Photo Approved | High-resolution parental photo verified; face unobstructed, good lighting.     |
|                   | Tool: AdminOrders / AdminStudio Photo Vault Inspector.                         |
+-------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2. MCS Locked     | Master Character Sheet generated; 3 poses inspected; likeness approved.        |
